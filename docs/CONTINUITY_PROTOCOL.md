# Continuity Protocol — Read Every Session

1. Before answering or editing, read `README.md`, `STATUS.md`, `PROJECT_NOTEBOOK.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, and any relevant files.
2. Treat repository documents as the durable source of truth; chat history may be incomplete. Do not invent progress or assume a task is done.
3. Preserve meaningful brainstorming, niche ideas, alternatives, reasoning, failed attempts, constraints, open questions, and hypotheses. Put untested ideas in `IDEAS_BACKLOG.md`; do not silently discard them.
4. Teach while building in small steps. Be concise; explain unfamiliar technical/finance terms in plain language (in brackets on first use). Say what a step does and why it matters.
5. Distinguish verified facts, inferences, and untested hypotheses. Never claim a file, test, commit, or result exists unless verified.
6. After substantive progress, update the relevant files, including `STATUS.md`; read back the saved content to verify it. Report exactly which files changed and any verification performed.
7. End substantial sessions with: completed, decisions, new ideas/questions, blockers, and next single action.
8. Protect research validity: chronological splits, no future information, baselines, honest metrics, documented assumptions, no profitability guarantees.
9. Keep the PRD scope visible. If a change conflicts with it, explain the trade-off and record the decision rather than silently changing scope.
